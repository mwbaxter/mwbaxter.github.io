Privacy policy

Assistant V2 · last updated 3 October 2026

Who uses it

Assistant V2 is a personal app used only by its owner, who also built it. It isn't available to anyone else.

What it reads from Google

When its owner signs in with Google, it can use:

  • Gmail: reading email, and on the home computer labelling it and marking it read, to answer questions, sort the inbox and find travel bookings.
  • Google Calendar: reading events, and adding or changing events when the owner asks.
  • Google Contacts: reading contact details to look people up.

How it's used

Only to do what the owner asks or has set up, such as briefings, reminders and travel plans. It isn't used for advertising, sold or shared with anyone.

Where it's kept

On the owner's iPhone, their home computer and a database in the owner's own cloud account. Google sign-in tokens are stored in the iPhone's Keychain and on the home computer.

AI processing

Requests may be handled by AI models on the iPhone itself, on the owner's home computer, or by Google's Gemini API under the owner's own account.

Google's policy

Assistant V2's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in your Google Account under Security, in the list of third-party apps with access to your account, or by disconnecting Google in the app's settings.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
